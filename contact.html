FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is the counselling really free?

Yes. The first consultation on call or WhatsApp is free. We tell you the options and the full cost before you start any paid service.

How long does a Gulf work visa take?

It depends on the employer and country. Many UAE and Saudi work visas take a few weeks after the offer and documents are complete. We give you a realistic timeline for your case.

Do you guarantee a visa?

No one can honestly guarantee a visa. Decisions are made only by the embassy or immigration authority. We make sure your application is complete and correct to give it the best chance.

What documents do I need first?

A passport with at least 6 months validity, passport photos, education and experience certificates. We share a full checklist for your country and job.

Can I apply if I do not have a passport yet?

Yes. Apply for your passport first through Passport Seva. We can guide you on the process while you wait.

Which countries do you cover?

We guide on jobs and visas worldwide, including the Gulf, Europe, UK, Canada, USA, Australia, New Zealand and Asia. If your country is not listed, message us and we will check.

Do you help with student visas?

Yes. We guide you on university options, admission letters, visa forms, financial documents and interview preparation for the USA, UK, Canada, Australia and Europe.

Planning to work, study or settle abroad?

Send your passport details and work experience on WhatsApp. Our team will tell you which job and visa options fit your profile.
