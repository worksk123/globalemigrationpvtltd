About Global immigration

We help people from India build careers abroad, with clear advice and step-by-step support.

Who we are

Your partner for overseas careers

Global immigration is an overseas job and visa consultancy for countries worldwide, including the Gulf, Europe, UK, Canada, USA and Australia. We guide workers, professionals and students through jobs, documents, visas and travel.

Many people lose money and time because they do not understand the process. Our goal is to make every step clear: what documents you need, what each stage costs, how long it takes and what happens after you land.
